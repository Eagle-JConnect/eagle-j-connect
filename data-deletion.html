Eagle-J Connect — Data Deletion

Eagle-J Connect respects your privacy. You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of our website or Facebook Login.

How to request deletion

  1. Email our support team using the contact email published on the Eagle-J Connect website.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us which account or Facebook Login connection you want removed. Do not send your password.

We will review your request, may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity, and will delete the associated data we control when appropriate, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Disconnect Facebook

You can also remove Eagle-J Connect from your Facebook account under Facebook Settings, then Apps and Websites. Disconnecting the app does not necessarily delete data already stored by Eagle-J Connect, so please contact us to request deletion of that data.

Contact

To request deletion, contact the support email published on the official Eagle-J Connect website.
