Data Deletion Request

Eagle-J Connect respects your privacy. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information that we control.

How to submit a request

  1. Email ingjudesversanne@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject “Eagle-J Connect Data Deletion Request”.
  3. Tell us the account email address or username associated with your account. Never send your password or full payment credentials.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account.

What happens next?

We will review the request and take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize information we control within a period required by applicable law. Some information may be retained when legally required or permitted, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or compliance. We will explain any material limitation where appropriate.

Third-party sign-in

If you used Google or another external sign-in provider, you may also revoke its access from that provider's account settings. Revoking access alone does not necessarily delete information already held by Eagle-J Connect; submit the request above as well.
