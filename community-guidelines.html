Community Guidelines

Effective date: October 13, 2026

These guidelines help keep Eagle-J Connect useful, respectful, and trustworthy for people discovering opportunities and promoting legitimate businesses, products, services, and jobs.

1. Be honest

Use accurate names and descriptions. Do not impersonate others, fabricate qualifications, misrepresent products, or publish fake jobs, businesses, reviews, or offers.

2. No scams or illegal activity

Do not use the platform to facilitate fraud, phishing, money-laundering, illegal goods or services, deceptive investment promises, or other unlawful conduct.

3. Respect others

Threats, targeted harassment, hateful abuse, discriminatory content, sexual exploitation, and publishing another person's private information without authorization are not allowed.

4. Respect intellectual property

Only upload photos, logos, videos, text, and other material that you own or are authorized to use. Do not copy protected content or falsely claim another person's work as your own.

5. Protect personal information

Do not post passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, private contact details, or confidential information in public listings or messages.

6. No spam or platform abuse

Do not flood the platform with repetitive posts, manipulate search or engagement, distribute malware, probe accounts without authorization, or attempt to bypass security controls.

7. Jobs, products, and services

Describe conditions and prices honestly. Do not request unlawful fees from job seekers or make unsupported claims. Users should independently verify offers and use appropriate caution before sharing information or sending money.

8. Reporting and enforcement

Report suspicious or harmful content through available platform contact channels. Eagle-J Connect may review reports, remove content, limit features, or suspend accounts where reasonably necessary. We may refer credible threats or suspected unlawful activity to appropriate authorities where permitted or required by law.
