Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Eagle-J Connect is a platform designed to connect people, job seekers, employers, and businesses, particularly in Haiti and The Bahamas.

This Privacy Policy explains how Eagle-J Connect may collect, use, store, and protect personal information when people use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account details, business information, job-related information, and information you choose to submit through forms.

We may also collect technical information necessary to operate, maintain, and secure the platform.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Connect job seekers with employers.
  • Display approved business profiles and listings.
  • Review business submissions and prevent misuse.
  • Respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services.

3. Business Listings and Public Information

Certain information submitted for a business profile or public listing may be displayed to other visitors after approval. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information in public profile fields.

4. Third-Party Services

Eagle-J Connect may use third-party services to operate its website, manage accounts, store data, or provide login features. These services may process information as necessary to provide their functions and according to their own privacy policies.

If you choose to sign in using Facebook or another third-party service, information may be shared with us according to the permissions you authorize and the applicable service settings.

5. Facebook Login

If Facebook Login is offered, we may receive information authorized by you through Facebook, such as your public profile information and email address, when available and permitted.

We use this information to help create or manage your account and provide the features you request. We do not claim access to information you have not authorized Facebook to share.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about your personal information, request corrections, or request deletion of information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate security needs.

9. Children's Privacy

Eagle-J Connect is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or privacy practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
